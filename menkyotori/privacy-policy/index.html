MenkyoTori Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

AlightVN develops MenkyoTori (com.alightvn.menkyotori), an offline-first Android study app for Japanese driver's license written exams (Gentsuki, Karimen, and Honmen). This policy explains how the app handles your study data and how you can manage it.

On your device

MenkyoTori works 100% offline without requiring you to create an account or sign in. Your study progress—including completed lessons, mastered and review road sign statuses, the last viewed road sign per license category, and mock exam history—is stored locally in the app's private storage (Android DataStore) on your device.

Device-only preferences—including display language, selected license type, Furigana toggle, color theme style, and reading font size—are also kept privately on your device. Depending on your Android system settings, Android may include app data in its own device backup or transfer.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect a Google account in Settings, Google handles authentication and provides your Google account email address, which is displayed inside the app to show which account is connected. MenkyoTori requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope, allowing access solely to the app's own hidden application data folder inside your personal Google Drive. It cannot read, view, modify, or delete any other files in your Drive.

When you tap Back up now, MenkyoTori uploads a timestamped JSON snapshot of your study progress (completed lessons, mastered/review road sign statuses, last viewed road sign codes, and mock exam history) directly to that hidden folder over HTTPS. When you choose Restore latest backup, the app downloads the latest snapshot and smart-merges it with your local progress. Device-only preferences (display language, license type, Furigana setting, theme style, and font size) remain on your device and are not uploaded to Google Drive.

MenkyoTori does not operate any developer backend servers, does not display advertisements, does not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not sell or share your data with third parties. MenkyoTori's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

External search links

Optional study links (such as Ask Google on question explanations) open a search query in your device's web browser or Google app. Any websites or services you visit outside MenkyoTori operate under their own privacy policies.

Independent study app

MenkyoTori is independently developed by AlightVN for educational and exam-practice purposes based on Japan's Road Traffic Act (道路交通法). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing the National Police Agency of Japan (警察庁), prefectural driving license centers, or any government entity.

Retention and deletion

Your local study progress and preferences remain on your device until you clear them using Settings → Reset all progress, clear the app's storage in Android system settings, or uninstall the app. Manage any separate Android system backups in your device or Google account settings.

Google Drive backups stay in your Google account until you delete them or your Google storage settings affect their retention. You can open Google Drive Settings, choose Manage apps, and find MenkyoTori to delete its hidden app data. To revoke Drive access, tap Disconnect in MenkyoTori Settings or remove the connection in your Google Account connections.

Age and children's privacy

MenkyoTori is intended for users aged 16 and older preparing for Japanese driver's license exams. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
